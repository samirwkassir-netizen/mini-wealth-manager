# Screen recording plan, Mini Wealth Manager

You are shooting two things separately.

**The phone video is the master.** Read the whole script to camera, all seven beats, start to finish, without stopping. That gives me one continuous voice track for the full 90 seconds. Your face is only on screen for beats 1, 2 and 7, but I need the audio to run unbroken underneath everything.

**The screen recording is silent.** No talking, no mic needed. You are just producing footage I lay over the middle of your voice track. Nothing needs to sync. Take your time on every shot.

## Before you press record

**The site has to be pushed first.** The "What standing still costs" block is the shot beat 4 lands on and it is not live until you run Part 1. Load samirkassir.me/wealth, click "Show me an example first", and check that block is there before you record anything.

Then:

1. **Do Not Disturb on.** Control Centre, Focus, Do Not Disturb. A notification sliding in ruins the take.
2. **Chrome, one window, one tab.** Cmd+Shift+B to hide bookmarks. Close everything else.
3. **Zoom the page.** Cmd+plus twice, to 125 or 150 percent. Then hold your phone up next to the screen. If you cannot read the numbers at arm's length, zoom more. Most of your reach is on a phone.
4. **Fullscreen.** Cmd+Ctrl+F. Menu bar and dock disappear.
5. **Run the whole thing once without recording.** Know where every click is before the camera is on.
6. **Cmd+Shift+5**, Options: Show Mouse Clicks on, Timer 5 seconds, Save to Desktop. Microphone off. Record Entire Screen.

## Filming yourself on the phone

- **Vertical.** The final frame is 4:5, so portrait gives me the most face to work with.
- **Rear camera, not the selfie camera.** Big quality difference. Prop the phone, frame it, then step in.
- **Phone at eye level.** Not on the desk pointing up at you.
- **Light in front of you, not behind.** A window you are facing is the best light you own. A window behind you makes you a silhouette.
- **Plain wall or something quiet behind you.** Not a busy room.
- **Read the whole script through, one take.** Then do it twice more. Three full takes, that is it. The third is usually the one.
- **Do not read off the screen.** Learn the bold anchor lines, then say the rest however it comes out. If it sounds slightly different every take, that is correct.
